Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Visibility Tools is a Google Slides add-on that shows and hides slides automatically, based on tags you type in each slide's speaker notes. This policy explains what the add-on reads, what it stores, and where.

In short: Visibility Tools runs entirely inside Google's Apps Script platform, in your own Google account. The developer runs no servers, receives none of your slides, and uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

What the add-on reads

  • The presentations you use it in. It reads each slide's speaker notes to find visibility tags, reads each slide's title text to label slides in its reports, and reads and changes each slide's Skip slide setting to show or hide it. It also edits speaker notes when you use its tools to add or fix tags.
  • Only decks where you turned on automatic updates, when it runs on its own. If you turn on automatic updates in a deck, the add-on opens that deck about once an hour, under your account, to apply its tags.
  • Your email address. When you turn on automatic updates, the add-on records your email address in that deck so other editors can see who runs its updates (shown in the add-on's Settings, Help, and panel).

The add-on does not read your other files, your email, your calendar, or your contacts.

None of this data is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Data in your Google account can only be read by the add-on while it runs as you.

Error reports

If the add-on runs into an error, Google Apps Script records the error message in the developer's Google Cloud project so the problem can be fixed. Error messages describe what went wrong (for example, "Select one or more slides first") and may include a short value such as a setting you entered. The add-on never writes your slides or speaker notes into its error messages.

Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, share, or transfer your data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising, credit decisions, or training any model. Visibility Tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing the add-on and your data

  • To stop automatic updates for a deck, open Extensions > Visibility Tools > Settings in that deck and uncheck automatic updates. This removes its timer. If a deck is deleted or you lose access, its timer removes itself and the deck's stored settings the next time it runs.
  • To remove the add-on, open Extensions > Add-ons > Manage add-ons in Google Slides.
  • To revoke its access to your account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Tags in speaker notes are ordinary text in your decks; delete them like any other text.

Children

Visibility Tools is a tool for the people who manage slideshows, such as staff who run a school's or organization's display screens. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.
