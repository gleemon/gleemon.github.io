Slides that show and hide themselves

Visibility Tools is a Google Slides add-on for digital signage. Type a short tag in a slide's speaker notes, and the slide shows on the right days and hides when it's done.

How it works

TagWhat it does
[START: 2026-10-01]Hidden until this date, then shown.
[END: 2026-10-15]Shown through this date, hidden the day after.
[DAYS: MON, WED]Shown only on these days.
[GROUP: Sports]Rotating group: about a third of its slides show at a time, and every slide gets a turn.
[ALWAYS_SHOW] / [ALWAYS_HIDE]Forces the slide on or off.
[MANUAL]The add-on leaves the slide alone.

Hidden slides use Google Slides' own Skip slide setting, so they drop out of the published slideshow.

Features

  • Automatic updates. Turn them on once per deck and visibility stays current every hour, even when the deck is closed.
  • A panel beside your slides. See why the selected slide is showing or hidden, and change its dates, days, or group without typing tags.
  • Rotating groups. Fit more announcements than one loop can hold; groups reshuffle at the times you choose.
  • Review Tags. Finds misformatted dates, conflicting tags, and expired slides, and fixes what it safely can.

Get started

Install Visibility Tools from the Google Workspace Marketplace (link coming soon), open a deck, and choose Extensions > Visibility Tools > Open Panel.

Full instructions: Visibility Tools Guide

Your data

Visibility Tools runs inside your own Google account. The developer runs no servers and never receives your slides. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
