Common fixes

ProblemWhat to do
Slides didn't change after using a toolReload the browser tab. The Slides editor doesn't always redraw slides an add-on changed.
A slide shows or hides at the wrong timeOpen the panel and select the slide: it explains why. Check the time zone in Settings, and run Review Tags.
Automatic updates stoppedThey run under the account of whoever turned them on. If that person lost access to the deck, turn them on again from your account in Settings.
The display still shows a hidden slideGoogle republishes within a few minutes; the display shows the change the next time it reloads the published page.

When you email, include what you expected, what happened, and the slide number.
